# Selected References

- Bryant P, Pozzati G, Elofsson A. Improved prediction of protein-protein
  interactions using AlphaFold2. *Nature Communications* (2022).
- Evans R et al. Protein complex prediction with AlphaFold-Multimer. *bioRxiv*
  (2021). AlphaFold-Multimer v3 was used for the pilot.
- Mirdita M et al. ColabFold: making protein folding accessible to all.
  *Nature Methods* (2022). MMseqs2 was used for feature generation.
- Taschner M et al. Crystal structure of ODA16 and its interaction with IFT46.
  *Journal of Biological Chemistry* 292, 7462-7473 (2017).
- Wang L et al. Structural basis of human ODA16 and lack of detectable human
  ODA16-IFT46 binding in vitro. *Protein Science* 29, 1502-1510 (2020).
- Issa HB et al. DNAAF9/Shulin associates with IFT74/IFT81 in the context of
  outer dynein-arm transport. *Nature Structural & Molecular Biology* 32,
  2454-2464 (2025).
- Bhogaraju S et al. IFT74/IFT81 form an IFT-B module involved in tubulin
  binding. *Science* 341, 1009-1012 (2013).

The detailed evidence table and source notes used during planning are retained
outside the public repository record. Claims in this project distinguish direct
biochemical binding from co-immunoprecipitation, complex association, and
functional evidence.

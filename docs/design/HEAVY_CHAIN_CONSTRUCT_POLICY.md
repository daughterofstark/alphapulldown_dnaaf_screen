# Heavy-Chain Construct Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to the full-length axonemal dynein heavy-chain targets:

- DNAH5
- DNAH8
- DNAH9
- DNAH11
- DNAH17

## Decision

Full-length canonical targets are preserved in the verified UniProt dataset, but they are marked as `requires_construct_review` for executable first-pass AlphaPulldown screens.

No arbitrary domain truncations should be generated by the CPU-side preparation pipeline.

## Rationale

Axonemal dynein heavy chains are approximately 4,400-4,600 residues. Full-length multimer predictions can be computationally expensive, memory-intensive, and difficult to interpret. Domain-level constructs may be necessary, but residue boundaries must be justified by literature, structural annotations, AlphaFold monomer confidence, domain architecture, or expert review.

## Rules

- Preserve full-length canonical metadata and sequences.
- Do not invent residue ranges.
- Exclude heavy-chain prey pairs from first executable screens unless explicitly enabled.
- Keep future full-screen pair definitions annotated with `requires_construct_review`.
- Document every approved construct with canonical coordinates, rationale, source evidence, and reviewer.

## Current Status

The CPU preparation pipeline generates `data/processed/screens/heavy_chain_review.tsv` listing heavy-chain targets and their deferred status.
